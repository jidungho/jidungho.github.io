Epoch AI tracks the running record for the largest AI data center by IT power capacity — the power available to chips and servers, excluding cooling and other overhead — and finds it growing 2.3x per year since mid-2024, a doubling every ten months (90% CI: 8 to 13 months). The six record events run from Google's Papillion at 237 MW in August 2024, through xAI's Colossus 1 at 278 MW and the Anthropic–Amazon New Carlisle site at 398 MW in 2025, to Colossus 2 at an estimated 946 MW in June 2026. Five announced facilities would extend the record through 2028, but only the two arriving by early 2027 stay on trend; the three projected for 2028 — Meta Hyperion, a further New Carlisle expansion, and Microsoft's Fairwater Wisconsin at ~2,263 MW — land at roughly half the capacity the curve predicts, in the 2nd to 3rd percentile of the prediction interval. With only six record events, power estimates accurate to within about 1.4x, and dates uncertain by ±6 months, the authors caution that this is weak evidence for a slowdown rather than a break in the trend.
